## Document Scope & Purpose

This document details the long-term vision, Phase 2+ feature backlog, institutional expansions, and advanced technological integrations deliberately excluded from the SIH 2026 MVP to ensure focus on the core farm-to-buyer operating system.

---

## Planned Contents & Future Expansion Tracks

When fully detailed in subsequent phases, this document will contain:

### Track 1: Formal FPO Management System (Post-MVP)
- **FPO Entity Onboarding & Registration:** Legal entity verification, member farmer roster management, landholding registry.
- **Collective Ledger & Shared Inventory:** Warehouse inventory management, aggregate dispatch consolidation, and internal dividend/payout accounting.
- **FPO Bulk Commercial Contracting:** Large institutional contracts managed at the cooperative level.

### Track 2: Platform Operations & Administration CRM
- **User Verification & Risk Operations:** Field KYC verification, merchant business license auditing, fraud detection alerts.
- **Dispute Resolution Console:** Multi-party mediation tools, escrow hold management, and carrier arbitration.
- **Supply Chain Command Center:** Real-time macro supply monitoring, mandi price distortion alerts, and regional deficit interventions.

### Track 3: Financial Services & Banking Escrow
- **Licensed Escrow & Banking Partner API:** Regulated multi-party trust accounts with instant UPI AutoPay and NEFT/RTGS rails.
- **Agricultural Trade Credit & Invoice Financing:** Pre-harvest input credit and buyer 30-day payment term underwriting based on platform transaction reputation.
- **Parametric Crop Insurance Integration:** Weather-indexed micro-insurance triggered automatically by rainfall/heat anomalies.

### Track 4: Deep Logistics & Cold-Chain IoT
- **IoT Sensor Integration:** Real-time BLE/LoRa temperature and humidity telemetry in refrigerated transit trucks.
- **Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Fleet API Integration:** Automated job bidding and carrier exchange network with regional freight aggregators.

### Track 5: Institutional & Government Gateway Integration
- **National e-NAM Integration:** Direct interoperability with national APMC electronic trading platforms.
- **AgriStack & Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI):** Integration with India Digital Ecosystem of Agriculture (IDEA), Farmer Registry, and Land Record portals.
